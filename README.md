# Workforce Decision Hub

**Making workforce decisions simpler, smarter, and more scalable.**

An independent, AI-assisted application concept for managing workforce requests, operational decisions, and governance across different business environments.

> I don't build technology for the sake of technology. I build solutions to real business problems.

## The Business Problem

Workforce-related decisions are often distributed across spreadsheets, emails, messaging platforms, and disconnected approval processes. This can create limited visibility, inconsistent prioritization, delayed decisions, and weak auditability.

Workforce Decision Hub explores how these workflows can be centralized, standardized, and improved through accessible technology.

## What We're Building

An adaptable workforce operations application designed to support:

- Employee requests and status tracking
- Configurable approval workflows
- Manager and workforce management decision queues
- Rules-based validation and exception prioritization
- Decision history and audit trails
- Operational dashboards and performance monitoring

These are planned capabilities, not completed features.

## Initial Technology Stack

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Backend: Google Apps Script
- Data layer: Google Sheets
- Development approach: AI-assisted development with human-led business design, testing, and validation

## Why Start With Google Sheets?

The initial architecture intentionally uses accessible tools to explore rapid prototyping under infrastructure constraints.

The project will document trade-offs involving data volume, concurrent access, execution limits, maintainability, and performance. We will measure bottlenecks rather than assume their causes.
